Which Plan Really Works for a Shared Flat?

SubTV IP – the premium choice for flatmates

SubTV IP runs on an anti‑freeze server cluster with 99.9% uptime. That means you and your flatmates can watch different channels at the same time without buffering or crashes. The picture uses 4K / 60 fps HEVC, so it looks sharp on any large TV. Activation happens instantly after you pay – no waiting days. You can pay with PayPal or credit card, both secure. Need help? WhatsApp support answers quickly. It works on all the main devices: Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Samsung Smart TV (Tizen), LG webOS, Android TV Box, Xiaomi Mi Box S, and Apple TV. Use apps like TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, or Flex IPTV. To stop your internet provider from slowing down streams, set DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and increase buffer/cache to 5000 ms – steps you can do on your own device.

Generic budget resellers – why they fail in a shared flat

These cheap plans use overloaded servers. When multiple flatmates try to watch at the same time – especially on weekend evenings – the server cannot handle the load. You get constant buffering and low resolution (often 720p or worse). Activation is slow because it’s done manually. If something breaks, there is no real customer support. These plans look cheap but cost you time and frustration.

Expat / international server lines – high latency, low reliability

Some services route channels through servers far away. This adds high latency, so channels take a long time to start. Audio often drifts out of sync with video – very annoying for live sports or dramas. For a shared flat, where people watch different things at different times, this lag makes the service unusable.

VOD‑only providers – great for movies, bad for live TV

These services have huge movie libraries, but their live streaming technology is weak. They use cheap infrastructure that cannot handle live events. If your flatmates want to watch live sports, news, or real‑time shows, you will see frequent freezes and pixelation. They are not built for shared flats that rely on live TV.

How to Set Up IPTV in a Shared Flat (Simple Steps)

  • Choose a plan that supports multiple simultaneous connections. SubTV IP provides premium bandwidth – no per‑device limit for most plans.
  • Use a reliable player. TiviMate and IBO Player Pro are great for managing multiple playlists for different flatmates.
  • Prevent ISP throttling. Change your DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and set the buffer/cache to 5000 ms. This keeps streams smooth even during peak hours.
  • Split the cost. A 12‑month premium subscription is often the best value – divide the total among flatmates and pay once.
